# AnRu Typing v3.0 — 320-Level Touch Typing Studio (PWA + GitHub Pages Ready)
**Creator & Owner:** ANKESH KUSHWAHA

## What's Included in v3.0
1. **Clean Soft-White UI/UX + Simple Dark Mode Toggle**:
   - Designed around a calm, eye-friendly Soft White & Sky Blue interface (inspired by EdClub) with a single 1-click **Dark Mode / Light Mode** toggle button.
2. **FontAwesome 6 Icons Everywhere**:
   - Uses crisp FontAwesome 6 icons (`fa-solid`, `fa-regular`) across all navigation tabs, buttons, badges, and controls.
3. **320 Progressive Levels (10 Units × 32 Sheets)**:
   - Every key pair (`F & J`, `D & K`, `S & L`, `A & ;`, `G & H`, `E & I`, `R & U`, `T & Y`, `W & O`, `Q & P`, `V & M`, `B & N`, `C, X, Z`, Capitals, Punctuation, Numbers, and Code) has **8 to 16 dedicated practice sheets** (Key Intro, Isolated Key Anchor Drill, Word Builder, Alternating Rhythm, Precision Lock, and Speed Mastery).
   - Click **any key** on the Weak-Key Doctor heatmap (or click **"Practice Specific Key"** below the keyboard) to generate an instant intensive sheet for `U`, `R`, or any letter!
4. **Ready-to-Deploy GitHub Pages + Installable PWA**:
   - Includes `index.html`, `manifest.json`, `sw.js` (offline Service Worker), and vector app icons in `icons/`.

## How to Upload to GitHub & Make It an Installable PWA (3 Easy Steps)
1. Create a new public repository on GitHub (for example: `anru-typing`).
2. Upload all files from this folder (`index.html`, `manifest.json`, `sw.js`, `README.md`, and the `icons/` folder) to your repository root.
3. Go to **Settings -> Pages**, under **Branch** select `main` (root `/`) and click **Save**.
4. Open `https://<your-username>.github.io/anru-typing/` in Chrome on your **Phone** or **Laptop** and tap **"Add to Home Screen" / "Install App"**!
   - When sharing with friends, share `https://<your-username>.github.io/anru-typing/?friend=1` (or click **Friend View** in the top bar) so your personal **ANKESH KUSHWAHA** profile & certificate stay private.
